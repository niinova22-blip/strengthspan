Privacy Policy

Strengthspan · Last updated: October 27, 2026 · Bundle ID: com.strengthspan.app

Strengthspan helps you measure and train strength, balance and mobility. In short: Strengthspan has no server and no account. Your fitness data stays on your iPhone.

1. Who is responsible

Strengthspan is published by an independent developer. Contact: niinova22@gmail.com

2. Data the app processes

DataWhere it comes fromWhere it is keptWhy
Profile answers (age range, activity level, goals, available days, equipment, movement limitations you report, safety screening answers) You enter them in the appOnly on your deviceTo build a safe, suitable plan
Workouts, sets, weights, effort ratings (RPE), discomfort feedback You record them in the appOnly on your deviceTo adapt your plan and show progress

3. What we do not do

  • No user accounts, no sign-in, no servers operated by us.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Health and fitness data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for marketing.
  • Apple Health data is never used for advertising or data mining, as required by Apple.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. App Store analytics and crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous usage and crash statistics. These never include your fitness data.

6. Backups

Like other apps, Strengthspan's data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, under your control.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete: Settings › Privacy & data › Delete all my data permanently removes everything the app stored. Deleting the app also removes its data.
  • Permissions: camera and Apple Health access can be changed at any time in iOS Settings. The app keeps working without them (manual checks are always available).
  • Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot access, correct or export it for you; it exists only on your device.

8. Not medical advice

Strengthspan is a fitness and wellness app. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any medical condition, and its scores are not clinical measurements.

9. Children

Strengthspan is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 16.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

1. Veri sorumlusu

Strengthspan bağımsız bir geliştirici tarafından yayımlanır. İletişim: niinova22@gmail.com

2. İşlenen veriler

  • Profil yanıtları (yaş aralığı, aktivite düzeyi, hedefler, uygun günler, ekipman, bildirdiğiniz hareket kısıtları, güvenlik tarama yanıtları): yalnızca cihazınızda, size uygun ve güvenli plan oluşturmak için.
  • Antrenmanlar, setler, ağırlıklar, zorlanma (RPE) ve rahatsızlık bildirimleri: yalnızca cihazınızda, planı uyarlamak ve ilerlemeyi göstermek için.
  • Değerlendirme sonuçları (tekrar, süre, ölçüm güvenilirliği): yalnızca cihazınızda.
  • Kamera görüntüleri: yalnızca kamera ile değerlendirme sırasında ve izin verirseniz kullanılır. Kaydedilmez ve hiçbir yere gönderilmez; kareler cihazda bellekte işlenip hemen silinir, yalnızca sonuç sayıları saklanır.
  • Apple Sağlık verileri: yalnızca izin verirseniz okunur; tamamlanan antrenmanlar izin verirseniz Apple Sağlık'a yazılır.
  • Abonelik durumu: Apple App Store (StoreKit) tarafından yönetilir.
