Support

Questions, feedback or a problem with Strengthspan? Email niinova22@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Subscriptions

To cancel or change your subscription: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Strengthspan. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

All data stays on your iPhone. To erase it: Strengthspan › Settings › Privacy & data › Delete all my data. See the Privacy Policy.

Safety

Strengthspan is not medical advice. Stop if you feel unwell, and contact a health professional or your local emergency number if needed.
